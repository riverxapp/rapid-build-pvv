    Our history

    From a small family kitchen to a beloved neighborhood destination, Harvest Table Bistro has grown through a love of simple, honest food.

    2015: The beginning

    Founding

    Harvest Table Bistro began as a chef-led pop-up serving seasonal supper club dinners sourced from nearby farms and local producers.

    2018: A permanent home

    Milestone

    The restaurant opened its first brick-and-mortar location, introducing a full dinner menu and a warm, modern dining room.

    Today: Community favorite

    Present

    Now a trusted gathering place for families, friends, and celebrations, we continue to serve thoughtful food with heartfelt hospitality.

    About Harvest Table Bistro

    We create meals that feel both comforting and elevated, using seasonal ingredients and attentive service as our foundation.

    Our culinary approach blends classic techniques with a modern, approachable style. We work with local growers and producers whenever possible to keep the menu fresh, balanced, and inspired by the seasons.
